# The rubric is bound into the ruling, never into the plan

A rubric is hashed into the ruling it produces, so a conclusion can always be
re-checked against the criteria that produced it. It is deliberately absent
from the plan and from `input_hashes`, even though every other executable input
is bound there.

## Consequences

A plan describes what will be executed; a rubric describes how the results are
interpreted. Were the rubric bound into the plan, changing your mind about what
you value would make previously sealed evidence read `INCOMPARABLE` against new
runs — which would be false, since nothing about the measurement changed.

Keeping it out is what allows re-ruling over existing sealed bundles under new
criteria. That is most of the reason rubrics are a separate artifact at all.
